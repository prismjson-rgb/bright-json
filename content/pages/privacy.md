JSON Prism is designed with privacy in mind.

- **Local processing** — All JSON parsing, formatting, and viewing happens in your browser
- **No data sent by default** — Your JSON is never transmitted to our servers
- **Share links** — When you share, JSON is encoded directly into the URL fragment (client-side only). Nothing is uploaded
- **Optional short links** — If you click _Create short link_, the already-encoded payload (not your original JSON) is stored on our own Cloudflare KV for **30 days**, then auto-deleted. The short link service is never contacted unless you press that button

We do not collect, store, or sell your data.
